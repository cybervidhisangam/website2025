Regulating AI‑Generated Deepfakes & Misinformation:
Can the IT Amendment Rules, 2026 Do the Job?

Join this webinar to understand legal, policy, and practical implications of the 2026 amendments for platforms, creators, businesses, and users.

Why This Conversation Matters Today

Deepfakes and synthetic media now influence elections, reputations, and markets in India, creating an urgent need to understand what the new rules demand from platforms, creators, and businesses. The 2026 amendments to the IT Act have introduced India’s first formal definition and regulatory framework for Synthetically Generated Information (SGI), a category that now sits at the centre of public discourse on AI‑generated deepfakes, misinformation, and platform responsibility.

As platforms, creators, businesses, and users grapple with mandatory labelling, compressed takedown timelines, and uncertain liability boundaries, the need for clarity has never been more urgent.

This session brings together legal interpretation, policy context, and practical implications to help stakeholders understand what the law says, and what it leaves open.
