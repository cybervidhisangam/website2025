Why is this forum coming together?

Cybercrime is no longer confined to the realm of financial fraud or data breaches — it has become a force that undermines the very foundations of trust in our digital lives. At the same time, privacy, once taken for granted, is steadily eroding, leaving individuals, businesses, and governments grappling with questions of control and accountability. While regulation continues to play a vital role, it is clear that legal frameworks alone cannot keep pace with the speed and unpredictability of technological disruption. The real challenge lies in rebuilding trust; trust in systems, in institutions, and in the very idea that technology can serve society without compromising its values. This is where Cyber Vidhi Sangam - CLCF Conclave 2025 steps in: a forum where voices from law, technology, policy, and industry come together to examine these urgent questions, anticipate the challenges ahead, and shape the frameworks of tomorrow.

Cyber Vidhi Sangam 2025

Conclave on Law & Tech: Challenges Today, Solutions Tomorrow

On 24 October 2025, alumni of the PGD in Cyber Law & Cyber Forensics (PGDCLCF) from NLSIU, convene for the inaugural Cyber Vidhi Sangam - CLCF Conclave 2025. This is not a ceremonial gathering, but a working forum designed to address one of the most pressing questions of our time: How do law and technology evolve together to manage the risks of a digital-first world? We explore not just how to respond to cybercrime and privacy threats, but how to create a digital future that is innovative, fair, and secure.

Why This Matters Now?

As organizations digitize at scale, the risks of cybercrime, data breaches, and privacy failures are accelerating. Boards, regulators, and industry leaders are under pressure to anticipate threats that current frameworks barely address. For professionals working at the intersection of law, policy, and technology, the ability to navigate these challenges is no longer optional — it is career-defining. The conclave brings together leading alumni, industry experts, and academic voices for high-impact panel discussions and debates.

Panel Discussions and Fireside Chats

Building Trust in a Digital Era - the role of law in restoring confidence across stakeholders. Privacy in the Business Growth Context - balancing innovation, consumer trust and compliance. Emerging Threats in Cybercrime - understanding risks before they scale. These are not theoretical discussions; they are grounded in real-world cases, regulatory shifts, and business imperatives that shape the decisions of today and the careers of tomorrow.

A Platform for the Future

Cyber Vidhi Sangam is the foundation of a larger ambition: to create a techno-legal forum driven by practitioners who understand both the complexity of law and the velocity of technology. This platform will serve as a space for policy influence, industry collaboration, and continuous professional growth.

By joining the inaugural edition, participants position themselves at the forefront of a community that will shape India’s techno-legal landscape for years to come.

Missing this conversation means missing the direction in which the field is moving.

The Voice of Convergence

Why This Conclave, Why Now

India’s digital trajectory is accelerating - but its legal, governance, and cybersecurity frameworks remain fragmented. Cyber Vidhi Sangam 2025 is designed to address this gap with strategic clarity and institutional depth.

The keynote by Dr. Nigam Nuggehalli sets the tone. As Registrar-in-charge and Professor of Law at NLSIU, Dr. Nuggehalli brings a rare combination of academic rigor and systems-level insight. His address will frame the urgency of convergence - why law, cybersecurity, and governance must now operate as a unified strategic function.

This is not a ceremonial opening. Expect a keynote that challenges silos, elevates institutional responsibility, and positions ‘Cyber Vidhi Sangam’ as a platform for serious, future-facing collaboration.

DISCUSSION:

Privacy as Leverage — Building Trust, Loyalty, and Market Edge

As data protection frameworks evolve, privacy is no longer just a legal obligation — it’s a strategic differentiator. This session brings together legal minds, policy architects, and enterprise leaders to explore how privacy-first design can drive trust, loyalty, and market leadership.

  • Confluence Guide: Sidharth Chauhan, Assistant Professor of Law & Assistant Dean – Research, NLSIU

    A seasoned legal academic and institutional leader, Prof. Chauhan brings clarity and structure to complex regulatory themes. His work spans constitutional law, statutory interpretation, and legal education reform - making him an ideal anchor for a discussion on privacy in principle and in practice.

  • Policy Architect: Sachin Dhawan, Deputy Director, The Dialogue

    A leading voice in India’s digital policy ecosystem. At The Dialogue, he drives research and stakeholder engagement on data governance, privacy frameworks, and emerging tech regulation. His insights bridge legal nuance with policy pragmatism, especially in the context of India's evolving DPDP Act.

  • Data Protection Counsel: Shloka Narayanan, Of counsel, Poovayya & Co.

    Shloka Narayanan advises leading corporates on data protection, technology law, and regulatory compliance. With a sharp understanding of global privacy regimes and Indian enforcement trends, she helps clients turn legal obligations into strategic advantage - especially in high-risk, high-growth sectors.

  • Enterprise Privacy Leader: Sandeep Kumar Akkimolla, Director – Global CISO & DPO, ANSR

    Sandeep Kumar Akkimolla leads privacy, security, and compliance across Capgemini’s global operations. With deep experience in risk governance, data protection, and regulatory alignment, he offers a practitioner’s view of how privacy can be embedded into enterprise DNA - driving trust, resilience, and competitive edge.

CYBERLOGUE:

AI vs AI → The Future Between Cyber Defenders & Cyber Criminals

As artificial intelligence becomes both weapon and shield, this session brings legal, industry, and enforcement perspectives on the evolving arms race between cyber defenders and adversarial AI systems.

  • Session chair:

    Dr. A. Nagarathna, Associate Professor of Law & Co-Director, ACAR-DTCL, NLSIU

    A leading voice in cyber law and digital governance, Dr. Nagarathna brings academic depth and policy insight to the conversation. Her work spans intermediary liability, AI regulation, and digital rights, making her a pivotal moderator for this high-stakes dialogue.

  • Digital Trust Executive: Anuj Bhansali, VP – Trust & Safety, PhonePe

    Anuj Bhansali leads one of India’s most sophisticated digital risk infrastructures. With deep expertise in fraud detection, platform integrity, and adversarial threat modeling, he offers a frontline view of how AI is deployed to protect millions of users in real time.

  • Innovation Leader: Rahul Sasi, Co-Founder & CEO, CloudSEK

    Rahul Sasi is a globally recognized cybersecurity entrepreneur and ethical hacker. At CloudSEK, he spearheads AI-driven threat intelligence solutions that preempt cyberattacks across sectors. His insights bridge technical innovation with strategic foresight.

  • Law Enforcement Member: Dr. Fakkeerappa Kaginelli, IPS, DIG APSP Battalion

    Dr. Kaginelli brings operational intelligence from the field - where cybercrime, AI misuse, and digital forensics collide. With years of investigative experience, he anchors the enforcement lens on emerging cyber threats.
